CHive App Privacy Policy

How information is collected, used, shared, and protected when you use CHive, the companion app for customers who bought their smartphone on an installment plan through a CHive partner.

1. Introduction

Who we are. CHive Technology ("CHive", "we", "us") operates the CHive mobile application (the "App"). CHive is the companion app for customers who bought their smartphone on an installment plan through a CHive partner.

What the App does. The App keeps you up to date on your installment plan, helps you pay on time through reminders and in-app payments, and lets you reach support when you need it. The App also supports device management features linked to your installment agreement (see Section 6).

Purpose of this policy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how we protect it, and the rights you have.

Applicability. This policy applies to customers who use the App on their purchased device. Information collected about you by an agent at the time of purchase is covered by the CHive POS Privacy Policy; where that information is shown or used in this App, this policy also applies.

Consent. By installing, activating, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. Where the App is part of a credit agreement, some processing is required to perform that agreement. If you do not agree, please contact us before using the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

Account and login information

  • Registered mobile number and/or email address
  • One-time passwords (OTPs) or PIN, verified and not stored in readable form
  • Language and notification preferences

Support and communication

  • Messages, queries, and complaints you send through the App
  • Attachments you choose to share, such as screenshots or payment receipts

2.2 Information linked to your purchase

This information is collected by the agent at the point of sale and made available in the App so you can manage your account.

Identity and contact details

  • Full name, mobile number, email address, date of birth
  • Residential address

Purchase and credit details

  • Order ID, device model, and purchase date
  • Credit amount, down payment, tenure, interest or charges, and repayment schedule
  • Outstanding balance, due dates, and overdue status

Payment information

  • Payment amounts, dates, methods, and status
  • Transaction IDs and payment references
  • Payment proof images you upload

We do not store your full card numbers, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords. These are handled directly by our payment partners.

2.3 Information collected automatically

Device identifiers

  • IMEI number(s), device serial number, and SIM details (such as SIM slot status and carrier), used to link the device to your purchase and protect it against misuse
  • Device model, manufacturer, operating system, and version
  • App version and push notification token

Device status

  • Whether the device is online, its lock status, and the last time it connected to our servers
  • Events such as SIM change, factory reset attempts, or App removal attempts

Location data

  • Approximate or precise device location, only where you grant permission or where required under your credit agreement for security and recovery purposes

Usage data

  • Screens viewed and actions taken in the App, crash logs, and performance data

We do not access your personal photos, contacts, messages, call logs, or browsing history.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Verify your identity and keep your account secure
  • Show your purchase, credit, and repayment details
  • Process payments and confirm payment status
  • Send payment reminders, due-date alerts, receipts, and service notifications
  • Link the device to your purchase using its device identifiers
  • Apply device management actions under your credit agreement, such as restricting and restoring device use
  • Detect and prevent fraud, theft, tampering, or misuse of the device or App
  • Respond to your support requests and complaints
  • Improve App performance and reliability using aggregated data
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and audit obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contractual necessity: to perform your purchase and credit agreement, including repayments and device management
  • Consent: for optional permissions such as notifications and location, which you can withdraw in device settings
  • Legitimate interests: to prevent fraud and protect the device while it is under a credit agreement
  • Legal obligation: to meet requirements under applicable law of your Country

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We share your data only as needed to provide the App and your credit service:

  • Lending and financing partners that fund or service your purchase, for repayment tracking and collections
  • Payment partners and gateways, to process and verify your payments
  • The store or agent where you bought the device, for order and support purposes
  • Service providers that host our servers, send notifications, or provide analytics and crash reporting, under confidentiality obligations
  • Affiliated group companies of CHive, for the purposes in this policy
  • Regulators, courts, and law enforcement, when required by law

We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Third-party services and SDKs

The App uses third-party services for push notifications, payment processing, location services, device management, secure storage, crash reporting, and network communication. These providers process data only on our instructions.

International data transfers

Our data is primarily stored and processed in India. If data is transferred outside India, we apply appropriate safeguards as required by law.

Device restrictions for credit purchases

Because you bought the device on an installment plan, your agreement may allow us to restrict device use when payments are overdue. Before any restriction, we send reminders through the App and/or SMS. While restricted, the device can still make emergency calls and open the App to make a payment.

Restrictions are removed automatically once the overdue amount is paid. When your credit is fully repaid, device management features are permanently released, and you may uninstall the App.

7. Data Storage and Security

On-device storage. Sensitive data such as session tokens is stored using encrypted, secure storage on the device.

Data transmission. All data exchanged between the App and our servers is sent over secure HTTPS connections.

Access controls. Access to customer data is limited to authorised personnel and partners who need it to provide the service.

Data retention. We keep personal data for as long as your account or credit agreement is active, and afterwards only as long as required by law, regulation, or to resolve disputes. After that, data is deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of data that is no longer needed
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing, such as notifications or location
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity
  • Lodge a complaint with us or with the Data Protection Board of India

Some data cannot be deleted while your credit agreement is active or while we are legally required to keep it. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details in Section 12.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for customers who are 18 or older and have entered into a purchase agreement. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child's data has been shared with us, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published in the App or on our official website, and we will notify you in the App of any significant changes.

11. Grievance Redressal

For any privacy concern or complaint, contact our Grievance Officer using the details below. We will acknowledge your complaint and aim to resolve it within the timelines required by law.
